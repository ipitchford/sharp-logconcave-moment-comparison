## Permitted contribution language

The proposed contribution is the remaining real-exponent optimisation in the variance-normalised problem, including strict exclusion of other interior parameters and the full transition classification. The reduction itself and its real-exponent scope are already known. The manuscript supplies a **self-contained, equality-sensitive proof of the reduction**; this is an exposition and assurance contribution, not an established first proof. Invoking an established theorem does not make a prior version conditional. No unconditional-priority or first-written-proof claim is justified.

At the transition there are two extremal families after scaling and reflection, not three equivalence classes. No irrationality of the transition point is established. At p=2 every admissible law has ratio one. Neither the general two-exponent problem nor quantitative distributional stability with an explicit rate is claimed solved. The added fixed-exponent qualitative stability corollary is a compactness consequence of the equality classification and established log-concave convergence results, not a claim to a new general stability method. Numerical scans and higher precision do not establish these exclusions: the proof requires the analytic arguments and successful production enclosures.

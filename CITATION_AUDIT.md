## Current attribution decisions

- MRTT posed the precise centred, variance-normalised problem and numerically predicted the transition. The candidate's claimed contribution is a proof of the remaining real-exponent optimisation and equality classification, not discovery of the conjecture or approximate phase transition.
- Eitan already covers real exponent pairs and contains equality-sensitive reasoning. A detailed self-contained exposition is valuable but does not justify “first written proof” or “unconditional now”.
- Crossing/Chebyshev methods have antecedents; the package does not claim their invention.
- Even p>2 is established prior work. The package's full statement includes that range but does not claim it as newly discovered.
- A decimal p* does not establish irrationality. Reflection identifies the two oriented centred exponentials, leaving two families at p*.
- Related non-centred comparisons or exponential-sum lower bounds are not the same optimisation problem.
